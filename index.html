FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registering, govt statutory fees, and running a UK Ltd company.

What are the official Companies House government fees (+Govt Fee)?

The statutory fee charged directly by UK Companies House is £100 for digital incorporation, and £50 for the annual Confirmation Statement (CS01). We maintain 100% transparent pricing so you always know your exact breakdown without hidden charges.

Can I register a UK company if I am not a resident in the UK?

Yes, absolutely. UK law allows foreign nationals and non-residents to 100% own and direct a UK Ltd company without living in the UK or visiting in person.

How do I use your online HMRC Calculators?

You can click on any calculator card in our HMRC Calculators Suite to directly estimate Corporation Tax brackets, VAT liabilities, and PAYE salary breakdown instantly.

Can I open a Stripe and Wise Business account with this setup?

Yes. With the official Certificate of Incorporation, UK Registered Address, and HMRC UTR number, you are fully eligible to apply for Wise Business, Payoneer, and verified Stripe/PayPal accounts.
