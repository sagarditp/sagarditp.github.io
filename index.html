FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registering and running a UK Ltd company.

Can I register a UK company if I am not a resident in the UK?

Yes, absolutely. UK law allows foreign nationals and non-residents to 100% own and direct a UK Ltd company without living in the UK or visiting in person.

How long does the UK incorporation process take?

Company registration at Companies House typically completes within 24 to 48 working hours once identity verification is submitted.

Can I open a Stripe and Wise Business account with this setup?

Yes. With the official Certificate of Incorporation, UK Registered Address, and HMRC UTR number, you are fully eligible to apply for Wise Business, Payoneer, and verified Stripe/PayPal accounts.

What annual tax filings are mandatory for my UK Ltd company?

Every UK Ltd must submit an Annual Confirmation Statement (CS01) to Companies House and an Annual Corporation Tax return (CT600) with statutory accounts to HMRC. Our team manages both end-to-end.
